PURDUE RCAC · JUL–SEP 2026

CyberFaCES SmartSearch evaluation

Built evaluation harnesses for a teammate-led course-search application to test whether more complex retrieval pipelines actually improved relevance.

Nine experiments informed a decision to defer reranking

My contributions & impact
  • Ran isolated Kubernetes Jobs requesting NVIDIA H100 GPUs on Anvil, moving experiments out of the live serving pod after an out-of-memory failure.
  • Compared BM25, vector search, reciprocal rank fusion, and rerankers using NDCG@10, MRR, precision, and recall. Evaluation-set sizes varied by experiment.
  • On 1,035 ground-truth queries, plain vector search achieved NDCG@10 of 0.5241 and beat every reranked variant. LLM query expansion raised BM25 from 0.4159 to 0.4469 and improved all four metrics.
  • Found a GPU-placement bug: one run took six hours and four minutes without proper GPU placement versus 91 seconds on GPU. Flagged a related risk in the production reranker loader.
  • Reproduced all nine experiments and wrote a runbook with troubleshooting, verification, and per-experiment steps.
  • The findings prompted follow-up experiments and a decision to hold off on reranker investment until quality improved.

PyTorch / CUDA · H100 · Kubernetes Jobs · Retrieval evaluation
